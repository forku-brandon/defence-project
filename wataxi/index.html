Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

You can book a ride through our mobile app, by calling our dispatch center, or using the booking form on our website. App bookings are the fastest and most convenient option.
We accept cash, credit/debit cards (through the app), and mobile payments. Corporate accounts can be set up for monthly billing.
Fares are calculated based on distance traveled and time spent in the vehicle. There's a base fare that covers the first portion of your trip, then a per-mile/per-minute rate after that. You'll always see the estimated fare before confirming your ride.
Yes! You can schedule rides up to 30 days in advance through our app or by calling our dispatch center. This is especially useful for airport transfers or important appointments.
All our drivers undergo thorough background checks and regular training. Vehicles are inspected frequently, and our app allows you to share your ride details with friends/family. We also offer a Night Safe service with additional safety features.
